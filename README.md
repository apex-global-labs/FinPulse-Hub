# FinPulse Hub

A finance-focused calculator website planned around high-intent, genuinely useful financial calculations. The project combines the former **Financial Intelligence Hub** and **Business & Professional Suite** concepts. The former **Universal Online Tools** division is explicitly out of scope.

## Project status

**Stage:** Product blueprint and requirements  
**Website:** Not launched  
**Hosting target:** GitHub Pages (free static hosting)  
**Stack target:** HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript; no paid services required for the initial release  
**Current release:** Planning only — no production calculators have been implemented yet

## Product scope

FinPulse Hub will focus on:
- Loans, EMI, amortization and mortgages
- Savings, interest, budgeting and financial planning
- Freelance income and professional pricing
- Business finance, profit, margin, break-even and cash flow
- Tax, salary and VAT/GST estimates where country rules are verified
- Currency conversion and foreign-exchange transfer-cost education
- Limited cryptocurrency unit/value and fee arithmetic for education only
- Property and accounting calculations

This is not a broker, exchange, wallet, investment advisory or trading platform. Forex and crypto tools will not provide trade execution, trading signals, leverage/margin or position-sizing features, investment recommendations, or buy/sell prompts.

## Operating principles

1. Correct formulas and transparent assumptions come before calculator count.
2. Each calculator must solve a distinct user task and search intent.
3. No country-specific tax or legal result is published without reliable, dated sources and review.
4. User-entered data is processed locally in the browser wherever possible.
5. No paid API, paid hosting, premium dependency or custom domain is required for the first launch.
6. Do not claim a feature is complete, tested or live until the relevant evidence exists.
